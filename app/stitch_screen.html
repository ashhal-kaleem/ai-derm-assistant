How to Capture an Accurate Lesion Photo

Diagnostic accuracy depends critically on focal distance, ambient diffuse illumination, and lesion isolation.

wb_sunny
Rule 01

Diffuse, Indirect Lighting

Avoid strong direct flashlight glare. Daytime window light without direct sun rays yields optimal skin contrast.

center_focus_strong
Rule 02

Macro Distance (10–15 cm)

Hold lens roughly four inches away. Ensure camera autofocus locks firmly on the lesion edge.

straighten
Rule 03

Include Reference Scale

For maximum millimeter precision, place a coin or metric ruler 1 cm alongside the mole.
